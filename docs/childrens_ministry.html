Children Ministry

"Children are an heritage of the Lord."(Psalms127:3).With these blessings the responsibilities are also added. As stewards of God's children, parents are responsible for helping children grow physically, intellectually, emotionally and spiritually. In our ministry we provide an environment in which children are safe, are taught at an age-appropriate level and are encouraged to develop their relationship with Jesus Christ.

We minister to the spiritual needs of our children through a children's ministry program"Kids Zone". Nowadays many children reach adulthood and decide not to follow Christ because of their environments the ways they have walked and moreover they are not prepared themselves to accept Jesus Christ as their Savior. The main thing is we do not have a program to help these children to receive Jesus into their lives. So God gave a plan to Rev. Isaiah to turning this type of children's life to His Glory. "PREMALAYA MINISTRIES" is working wholeheartedly to give a better and healthy future to those children. The purpose of this kids Zone is to develop child's focus on GOD. This Kids Zone will not only give a general introduction about God, it will also give the following training to overcome the teenage issues and turning our kids to the Lord

  • How to Protect ourselves from evil
  • How to receive Divine Wisdom
  • How to Prosper in life
