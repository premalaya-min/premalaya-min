About Premalaya Ministries

A registered Religious, Educational and Social Trust reaching the unreached through the Love of Christ.

Premalaya Ministries is a registered religious, educational and social trust born out of the vision received from the Lord to Rev. Dr. A. Isaiah in the year 2005. It is also registered under 12AA of the Income Tax Act and the Foreign Contribution (Regulation) Act (FCRA) of the Government of India. Premalaya Ministries and Premalaya Baptist Church have been used by the Lord to expand HIS Kingdom in this world. Premalaya has never hesitated to go ahead in faith despite trials and tribulations since its inception.
