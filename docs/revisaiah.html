Rev. Dr. A. Isaiah

Founder, Premalaya Ministries

Rev. Dr. A. Isaiah engaged in church activities from his childhood days. He personally served in Children's Ministry as a Sunday school teacher. He then became a youth leader and led youth towards the Cross. He was baptized on 03.03.1985. Later, he became a deacon and served as an elder of the Church.

As an Evangelist with an ambition to preach the Word of God and to reach the unreached, he has done ministry in 270 remote villages in Kanigiri and Nellore Districts of Andhra Pradesh and in Chennai and Tiruvallur Districts of Tamil Nadu. He has carried out all these activities through Christ who strengthens him.

After completing his Master of Commerce (M.Com), he joined a Theology course and obtained a Master of Divinity (M.Div) to the glory of God. Later, he studied Bachelor of Law (B.L). Recognizing his service in ministry and social work, he was conferred Doctorates (Doctor of Divinity) by the International Good Shepherd Theological Open University, a unit of Universal Telugu Christian Foundation and Christ Centered Ministries U.S.A., ATA, and University of Jerusalem.
